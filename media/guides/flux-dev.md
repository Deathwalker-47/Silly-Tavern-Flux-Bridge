Order: subject and action, setting, lighting, then format (photo, selfie, film still).
Plain visual phrases, comma separated, under 80 words.
Say the framing: close-up, waist-up, full body, from above.
Name real light: window light at dusk, a phone flash, neon from the street.
Leave identity to the LoRA. Don't list facial features unless the Appearance text gives them.
A negative prompt is rarely needed. Leave it empty unless something must be excluded.
